## Design decisions supplied by the approved topic

The following are workflow contracts, not empirically established claims: a source-to-claim ledger; grade-8 target; mandatory analogy boundaries; translation target confirmation; permission preservation; proposal-versus-runtime distinction; and the pass/fail fixture rubric. ## Thin evidence and conflicts

The sources support clear language, reader context, consistent terminology and bounded explanation. They do not supply a validated grade-8 comprehension score, a universal analogy, or software-specific teach-back efficacy.
