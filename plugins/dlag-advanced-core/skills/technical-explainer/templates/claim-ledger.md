# Claim ledger

Record source identity and an accessible line or section location. Never copy secret values.

| Claim | Source location | Meaning preserved | Kind | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Source section or line | Actor, action, condition, or status | Source claim, inference, or analogy | What remains unverified |

Every factual assertion needs source support. Analogies are illustrative. An unsupported assertion is removed or listed as an unknown.
