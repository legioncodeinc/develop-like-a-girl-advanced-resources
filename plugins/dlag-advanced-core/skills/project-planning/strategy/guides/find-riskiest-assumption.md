# Find and plan the riskiest assumption test

Fills section 8. Questions: Batch 7.

## Steps

1. **Collect assumptions** from every earlier section: "how we know" answers that were beliefs, bet reasons, the north-star link to revenue, v1 feasibility, and who pays.
2. **Phrase each as something that must be true,** and make it specific. "Freelancers will connect their bank account in the first session" beats "users like it" (Product Talk, Assumption Testing (2023)).
3. **Tag the type:** desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, or ethical (Product Talk, Assumption Testing (2023)). Do not argue over the label; the list exists to surface risk. Give viability extra attention: SVPG says it was long underrated, and for AI products it covers cost, pricing, liability, and ethics (SVPG, Strong Opinions Loosely Held (2026)).
4. **Rate importance and evidence.** The riskiest assumptions are the ones that matter most and have the least proof (assumption mapping) (Product Talk, Assumption Testing (2023)).
5. **Pick one riskiest assumption** with the owner. Recommend the high-importance, no-evidence one.
6. **Plan the smallest test.** Build only enough to test that one unknown, not a whole product (the Riskiest Assumption Test idea) (Hackernoon, Riskiest Assumption Test (2016)). Test one assumption, not the whole idea (Product Talk, Assumption Testing (2023)).
7. **Set pass and fail before the test.** Write the threshold and what you will change if it fails.
8. **Add an early warning signal** for each load-bearing assumption so you notice if it breaks later (Mind the Product, Three Products Three Failures (2026)).

## Common traps

- "People loved the demo" is not "people will pay". Treat those as two separate assumptions (Mind the Product, Three Products Three Failures (2026)).
- A competitor existing proves someone buys, but not which buyer or channel works for you (Mind the Product, Useful Is Not Enough (2026)).

## When to hand off

If the test needs interview scripts, an opportunity tree, or experiment design, hand off to the owner-designated discovery method (no separate Advanced specialist) and link its plan. This strategy method names the assumption and the pass line; discovery owns the method.

## Done when

An assumption table, one riskiest assumption, a smallest test with pass and fail lines, an owner, and a date.
