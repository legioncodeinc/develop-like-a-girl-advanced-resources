# Bounded work brief

- Outcome and approved plan: [specific result and acceptance criteria]
- Current owner: [registered namespaced capability; actual person or Specialist performing work]
- Method: [paired available Playbook and applicable local resources already read]
- Execution mode: [authorized dispatch with receipt, capable caller direct use, or read-only preparation]
- Tools and permissions actually available: [list; do not inherit an author's tools]
- Ownership: [exact paths and responsibility]; other workers' active paths: [exclusions]
- Inputs and project facts: [locations, versions, selected platform, unknowns]
- Shared boundaries: [provider/consumer, accepted revision, accepting authority, change protocol]
- Required outputs: [saved artifacts, findings, evidence references]
- Verification: [commands or inspections to perform, expected observations, actual executor]
- Candidate identity: [revision or saved-tree identity and included changes]
- Stop conditions: [missing inputs, failed boundary, repeated attempt, unsafe scope or deadline]
- Task start, deadline and reporting interval: [actual clock; no silent reset]
- Existing approval source and scope: [specific authorization; external/destructive effects separately gated]
- Completion return: [changed paths, exact results, failed attempts, unperformed work and next decision]

The recipient must read its method before work, preserve other owners' edits and report missing prerequisites. A brief is not evidence that the recipient started, finished or passed review.
